    SciCoproof Privacy Policy

    SciCoproof uses Google Sign-In solely to authenticate you and associate your proofreading runs with your account. We collect your email address, display name, and avatar URL from Google.

    Your uploaded LaTeX sources, Word documents, and text files are processed transiently to generate the proofread output and are not stored beyond your current session. Files are not shared with third parties, and are not used to train any model.

    Session tokens are stored in your browser's localStorage and expire after 30 days.
