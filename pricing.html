Questions

Why do I see prices in ₹ or $?

Prices are shown in your local currency based on where you're visiting from — ₹ for India and $ everywhere else. Your plan and every feature are identical regardless of currency.

What does the free plan include?

The free plan gives you one document per month, up to 5,000 words (or the equivalent). It includes Benjamin's context-aware grammar, unit, and notation checks with native tracked changes, at the same proofreading quality as the paid plans. Equation proofreading and 95+ language support are available on Pro and Max.

What counts as a "document"?

A single manuscript you submit for proofreading — a .tex project, a Word .docx, plain text / Markdown, or .rtf. Free allows one document a month up to 5,000 words; paid plans work off a generous monthly word budget instead — 70,000 words on Pro and 350,000 on Max.

What's the difference between Pro and Max?

Same features, same proofreading quality — the difference is volume. Pro includes 70,000 words per month (nearly unlimited for most researchers); Max gives you 5× that, 350,000 words per month, for heavy users and large groups.

Can I buy plans for my lab or institution?

Yes. Under Team & Enterprise you can purchase any combination of Pro and Max accounts at the same per-seat price, with no extra cost, managed under one centralized plan. Get in touch and we'll set it up.

Is the paid proofreading quality different from Free?

No — the proofreading quality is identical on every plan. Paid tiers don't proofread "better"; they unlock additional capabilities (equation proofreading, 95+ languages) and a much larger monthly word budget.

How do I pay, and can I cancel?

Secure checkout is coming via Razorpay, supporting UPI, cards, and net-banking in India and international cards worldwide. You'll be able to cancel anytime.
