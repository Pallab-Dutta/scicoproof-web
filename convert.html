Converting…
Comes through: sections, emphasis, lists, tables, footnotes, equations (as editable Word equations / LaTeX) and figures (PDF/EPS figures become PNG).
References: to Word, citations are formatted from the .bib in the style you pick, or numbered from the paper's reference list (.bbl / thebibliography). To LaTeX, Zotero/Mendeley/EndNote citations become \cite + refs.bib, and a typed or numbered reference list becomes a LaTeX bibliography with \cite in place of [1], [2, 3] or superscript numbers.
Doesn't: journal page layout (two columns, revtex/IEEE design), Word fonts and colours in LaTeX, TikZ drawings, and numbered cross-references (they become plain text).
